Modern Web Solutions for Knights of Columbus

"The pen is mightier than the sword, but modern knights require both modern swords and modern pens."

In today's world, effective communication through websites, email, and text messaging is essential for thriving councils. Cyberknight Websites provides the digital tools Knights of Columbus councils need to connect with members, share their mission, and grow their community in the modern age.

Reach Members Across Every Channel

Share announcements through your council website, text messages, and email—all from one place. The Cyberknight Secure Portal delivers your message across every channel so members never miss important updates, whether they prefer reading on the web, checking their phone, or opening their inbox.

Share Your Council's Events

Keep your members informed with dedicated event pages that showcase upcoming council activities, meetings, and community gatherings. Each event includes dates, times, locations, and descriptions, and is automatically published to your council website so members always know what's happening.

Know Your Members

View detailed member profiles with photos, contact information, officer positions, and council membership data. The Cyberknight Secure Portal imports member information directly from your member management system, giving council leaders an accurate, up-to-date directory at their fingertips.

One Portal for Everything

Manage meeting minutes, events, posts, forms, announcements, newsletters, members, billing, and your council website—all from a single secure dashboard. The Cyberknight Secure Portal is the central command center for council leadership, keeping every administrative tool in one easy-to-access place.
