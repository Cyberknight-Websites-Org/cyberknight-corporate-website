Modern Web Solutions for Knights of Columbus

"The pen is mightier than the sword, but modern knights require both modern swords and modern pens."

In today's world, effective communication through websites, email, and text messaging is essential for thriving councils. Cyberknight Websites provides the digital tools Knights of Columbus councils need to connect with members, share their mission, and grow their community in the modern age.

Share Your Council's Events

Keep your members informed with dedicated event pages that showcase upcoming council activities, meetings, and community gatherings. Each event can include dates, times, locations, and descriptions to ensure maximum participation and engagement.

Communicate Effectively

Share news, updates, and important information through council posts and announcements. With integrated email and SMS delivery powered by the Cyberknight Secure Portal, your messages reach members instantly, ensuring no one misses critical updates.

Manage Your Membership

Access a comprehensive directory of all council members using data imported from your member management system. The Cyberknight Secure Portal makes it easy to track membership, manage contact information, and maintain accurate records for your council leadership.

Streamline Administration

Store and automatically generate meeting minutes, facilitate Knights of Columbus form submissions, and create email newsletters featuring recent posts, upcoming meetings, links to the Columbia Magazine, and messages from your Grand Knight—all from one secure management portal.
