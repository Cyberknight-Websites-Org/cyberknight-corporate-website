## Who Am I

My name is Julian Lopez and I am a fourth degree knight of the Knights of Columbus and an officer of Council #2431 in Alhambra.

As an avid programmer, the duties of maintaining our website and other tech related tasks naturally fell to me. Over time, I started coding tools to help with these tasks, and the scale and quantity of them grew to the point that it would be nothing short of a waste if I kept them only to my council.

I created Cyberknight Websites in order to help other councils who don't have a tech expert such as myself. I am of the firm belief that in this era of digital communication, us Knights must not be left behind. It is to this end that I have made available this suite of resources to help brother Knights connect with members, share our mission, and grow our communities through effective websites, communication platforms, and other online means.

*"The pen is mightier than the sword, but modern knights require both modern swords and modern pens."*
