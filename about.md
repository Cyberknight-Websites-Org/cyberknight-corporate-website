My name is Julian Lopez and I am the founder and head of Cyberknight Inc. I am a fourth degree member of the Knights of Columbus and currently the Chancellor of council #2431 (Alhambra).

I started working on making a website for my council but quickly started to realize that I wanted to make something more versatile and useful than just that. 

Eventually I realized that what I had made was just too good to keep to just one council and that I could help other councils and organizations by providing them with the same resources that I had created for my council.
