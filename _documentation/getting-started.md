# Getting Started with Your Council Website

*This is a stub guide for demonstration purposes.*

## Introduction

Welcome to your new council website! This guide will walk you through the basic features and help you get started with managing your online presence.

## Accessing the Cyberknight Secure Portal

1. Navigate to [https://secure.cyberknight-websites.com](https://secure.cyberknight-websites.com)
2. Log in with your provided credentials
3. You'll see your dashboard with all available management tools

## Creating Your First Event

Events are a great way to keep your members informed about upcoming council activities.

**Steps:**
1. Click "Events" in the main navigation
2. Click "Create New Event"
3. Fill in the event details (title, date, time, location, description)
4. Click "Publish"

Your event will now appear on your public council website!

## Publishing a Post

Posts allow you to share news and updates with your council members.

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to "Posts" in the portal
2. Click "New Post"
3. Write your content using the editor
4. Add any images or attachments
5. Click "Publish"

Posts can be automatically included in your email newsletters.

## Managing Members

The member directory is automatically synced with your member management system, but you can view and update information through the portal.

## Sending Announcements

Announcements can be sent via email and SMS to all subscribed members.

**Important:** Always preview your announcement before sending to ensure accuracy.
