The Event Flyer Generator creates professional, AI-generated flyers for your events using the information already entered in the event form.

## Steps

### Step 1: Open the event editor and click "Generate AI Flyer"

Navigate to the event you want to create a flyer for, then click the blue "Generate AI Flyer" button in the Event Flyer section.

<img src="/assets/images/docs/generating-event-flyers/screenshot-01.png" alt="Event Flyer section with Generate AI Flyer button" class="announcement-img-lightbox">

### Step 2: Review and edit the flyer content

The system analyzes your event details and displays them in the flyer content editor. Edit the text as needed—each line becomes a separate element on the flyer.

<img src="/assets/images/docs/generating-event-flyers/screenshot-02.png" alt="Flyer content editor showing editable text and style options" class="announcement-img-lightbox">

### Step 3: Select a style and generate the flyer

Choose your preferred visual style from the dropdown menu (MS Word is selected by default), then click "Generate Flyer" and wait up to a minute for generation to complete.

### Step 4: Review your flyer and make revisions if needed

Once generated, you can review the flyer preview and either use it immediately or generate up to 3 revisions by changing the style or adding editing instructions.

<img src="/assets/images/docs/generating-event-flyers/screenshot-03.png" alt="Generated flyer preview with refinement options" class="announcement-img-lightbox">

### Step 5: Apply the flyer to your event

Click "Use This Flyer" to attach it to your event, then click "Submit Edits" at the bottom of the page to save all changes.

<img src="/assets/images/docs/generating-event-flyers/screenshot-04.png" alt="Event edit page showing the attached flyer with Download, Edit, and Remove buttons" class="announcement-img-lightbox">

## Editing an Existing Flyer (Optional)

After a flyer has been attached to an event, you can make changes by clicking the "Edit" button in the Event Flyer section. You can change the style, add editing instructions describing what changes you want, or both, then generate a revision.

<img src="/assets/images/docs/generating-event-flyers/screenshot-05.png" alt="Flyer editor showing options to revise the design" class="announcement-img-lightbox">

## Tips

- **Complete event details first** - The AI uses your event name, description, date, time, and location to create the flyer
- **Keep content concise** - Each line in the flyer content becomes a separate element, so shorter lines work better
- **Choose the right style** - Consider your event's tone and audience when selecting a visual style
- **Use revisions wisely** - You can generate up to 3 versions, so experiment with different styles and modifications
- **Remember to save** - Click "Submit Edits" at the bottom of the event edit page to save all changes
