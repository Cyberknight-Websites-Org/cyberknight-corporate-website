Quickly locate members by searching across multiple fields including names, email addresses, phone numbers, membership IDs, and officer positions. Search results display matched content with visual highlighting.

## Accessing the Search Page

Navigate to the Members Overview page, then click the Search button in the Member Actions card.

## Searching for Members

Enter a search term into the search field and click Search. Results will display all members matching your query across multiple fields.

## Viewing Search Results

Search results are displayed in a table showing the member name, a highlighted excerpt of the matching content, and membership information including officer position. Click the View Profile button to access the full member profile.

## Tips

- **Search multiple fields** - Queries search member names, email addresses, phone numbers, membership IDs, officer positions, and member notes simultaneously
- **Case-insensitive** - Search is case-insensitive, so "jose", "JOSE", and "Jose" all return the same results
- **Partial matches** - Search finds partial matches, so searching "albay" will find "Albayeros"
- **Membership numbers** - Search by membership number to quickly locate specific members
- **Officer titles** - Find members by their officer position like "Grand Knight" or "Treasurer"
