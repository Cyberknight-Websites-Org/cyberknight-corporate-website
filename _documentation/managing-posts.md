Posts allow your council to publish news and updates that appear on your council's website. You can write rich content, attach photos, and manage your post archive from a single place.

## Viewing the Posts Overview

The Posts Overview is your starting point for all post activity. It shows your most recent posts and provides quick access to create a new post or browse the full list.

## Creating a Post

### Step 1: Open the Create Post form

From the Posts Overview, click the **Create Post** button to open a new blank post form.

<figure>
<picture>
<img src="/assets/images/docs/managing-posts/screenshot-02.png" class="announcement-img-lightbox">
</picture>
<figcaption>The Create Post form with empty title and body fields</figcaption>
</figure>

### Step 2: Fill in the title and body

Enter a **Post Title** (up to 45 characters — a live counter tracks your progress) and write your content in the **Post Body** editor. The editor supports rich text formatting such as bold, italics, links, and lists.

<figure>
<picture>
<img src="/assets/images/docs/managing-posts/screenshot-03.png" class="announcement-img-lightbox">
</picture>
<figcaption>Post form with a title and body filled in, ready to be saved</figcaption>
</figure>

### Step 3: Save the post

Click **Create Post** at the bottom of the form. A loading indicator will appear while the post is saved. Once complete, you'll be taken to the edit page where you can add images.

## Editing a Post and Managing Images

After a post is saved, you can update its title and body, and manage attached images — up to 25 photos per post.

<figure>
<picture>
<img src="/assets/images/docs/managing-posts/screenshot-04.png" class="announcement-img-lightbox">
</picture>
<figcaption>Edit Post form for an existing post, showing the title and body editor</figcaption>
</figure>

To add an image, click **Add Image** in the Post Images section and select a file from your device (PNG, JPEG, JPG, WEBP, HEIC, or HEIF — up to 10 MB each). Each image can have an optional caption. Use the **arrow buttons** to reorder images, or click the **trash icon** to remove one. Click any thumbnail to preview the full image.

## Deleting a Post (Optional)

To permanently remove a post, open it in the edit form and click the red **Delete Post** button at the bottom of the page. This action cannot be undone.

## Viewing All Posts

The full post list displays all council posts in a searchable, sortable table. By default it is sorted by most recent first.

<figure>
<picture>
<img src="/assets/images/docs/managing-posts/screenshot-06.png" class="announcement-img-lightbox">
</picture>
<figcaption>All Posts list with sortable columns and column visibility toggles</figcaption>
</figure>

Use the **Columns To Display** checkboxes above the table to show or hide fields such as Created By, Last Edited, and Last Edited By. Click **Edit** on any row to open that post for editing.

## Tips

- **Save before adding images** — the image upload section only appears after a post has been saved for the first time
- **Use the character counter** — the title counter updates as you type to help you stay within the 45-character limit
- **Reorder images intentionally** — images display in the order shown in the edit form, so arrange them before saving
- **Caption your photos** — captions are optional but help readers understand what each image shows
