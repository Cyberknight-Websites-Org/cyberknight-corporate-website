Post search lets you find any post across your council without having to scroll through the full list. Results show the matched text highlighted in context alongside the post date and author.

## Searching for Posts

### Step 1: Open the Search Page

From the Posts Overview page, click **Search Posts**.

<picture>
<img src="/assets/images/docs/searching-posts/screenshot-01.png" class="announcement-img-lightbox">
</picture>

</figure>

### Step 2: Enter a Search Term

Type any word or phrase into the search box and click **Search**. The search matches against post titles, body content, author names, and image alt text — all case-insensitively.

<figure>
<picture>
<img src="/assets/images/docs/searching-posts/screenshot-02.png" class="announcement-img-lightbox">
</picture>
<figcaption>The Search Posts page ready to accept a query</figcaption>
</figure>

### Step 3: Review Results

Matching posts are listed with the date created, post title, author, and a highlighted excerpt showing where the match was found. Click **Edit** on any result to open that post.

<figure>
<picture>
<img src="/assets/images/docs/searching-posts/screenshot-03.png" class="announcement-img-lightbox">
</picture>
<figcaption>Search results for "breakfast" with the matched term highlighted in yellow</figcaption>
</figure>

## Tips

- **Partial words work** — searching "break" will match "breakfast"
- **Body text is searched** — the full post content is searched, not just the title
- **Results are sorted newest first** — the most recently created posts appear at the top
