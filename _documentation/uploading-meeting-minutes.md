Upload your council's meeting minutes as PDF files to keep them organized and make them searchable through transcriptions.

## Uploading a PDF

### Step 1: Navigate to the Meeting Minutes page

From your council dashboard, click **Meeting Minutes** in the navigation menu, then select **Upload PDF**. You can also reach the upload page directly from the Meeting Minutes Overview screen.

### Step 2: Choose a meeting date and file

On the upload page, select the date of the meeting using the date picker. Then click **Choose a file** and select the PDF meeting minutes file from your computer. Only PDF files are accepted.

<figure>
<picture>
<img src="/assets/images/docs/uploading-meeting-minutes/screenshot-02.png" class="announcement-img-lightbox">
</picture>
<figcaption>The upload form with date picker and file input</figcaption>
</figure>

### Step 3: Upload the file

Click the **Upload** button. Once uploaded, your minutes will appear on the Meeting Minutes Overview page in the Recent Meeting Minutes table.

<picture>

## Viewing Uploaded Minutes

Uploaded PDFs appear in the Recent Meeting Minutes section on the overview page. Click **View** to open the PDF directly in your browser. For access to the full historical record, click **View Full Archive** for a complete listing of all uploaded minutes by year and month.

## Tips

- **File type** — Only PDF files can be uploaded. If your minutes are in another format, convert them to PDF first.
- **Transcription** — After uploading, the system automatically generates a searchable transcription from the PDF. This may take a few minutes to process in the background.
- **Search** — Use the **Search** button on the overview page to search for keywords across all your meeting minutes.
